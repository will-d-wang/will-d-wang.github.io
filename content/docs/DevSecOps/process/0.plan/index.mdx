# Plan Secure Delivery

Planning connects a product outcome to architecture, risk, and evidence before implementation makes change expensive.

## Questions to resolve

- Which users and critical journeys are in scope, and how will success be measured?
- What data is collected, where does it move, and which retention or residency rules apply?
- Which trust boundaries, identities, dependencies, and abuse cases shape the threat model?
- What reliability, latency, capacity, recovery, and cost objectives apply?
- Who owns delivery, operation, incident response, and eventual retirement?
- Which acceptance criteria can be verified automatically, and which require judgment?

Record architecture decisions with alternatives, consequences, and revisit triggers. Decompose work into small, independently verifiable changes. Include telemetry, rollback or migration, documentation, and operational readiness in the definition of done.
