# Learn

This is a map through the notes on this site. Pick a discipline below, or follow the [build and operate a service lab](/labs/build-and-operate-a-service) if you learn best by doing. The sidebar groups existing notes by engineering practice, foundations, and archive topics without changing their URLs.

## Choose a path

- **[SRE](/docs/sre):** Observe a service, reason about reliability, and prepare for operational work.
- **[Infrastructure](/docs/infrastructure):** Understand the cloud and Kubernetes layers that run a service.
- **[DevOps](/docs/devops):** Build, test, and release changes with a short feedback loop.
- **[Platform Engineering](/docs/platform):** Make reliable delivery easier for other developers.

AI has its own [AI for Engineering](/ai) section, with a guided path and practical verification workflow.

## How to use the site

The [Labs](/labs) section gives you something to run. [Field Notes](/field-notes) explain the decisions and tradeoffs behind the work. Browse the Engineering Practice group for delivery, tools, security, and system design; use Foundations for networking, databases, programming, algorithms, and hardware. Graduate and career notes live under More from the Archive. Existing technical notes stay at their original URLs so bookmarks and links continue to work.
